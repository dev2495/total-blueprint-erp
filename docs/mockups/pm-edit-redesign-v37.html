POD + Packaging masters — do them next, not now
Honest recommendation on sequencing.
Do first · PM Edit (this redesign)
Highest leverage
Touched every time a new product gets added. Pain compounds daily. Fixing this unblocks the rest of the model.
Do next · Packaging master
Medium leverage
Inner pouch / gunny / sheet / tape / label / tag — kind bifurcation needs to flow through to the PM Edit's filter chips. Easier to redo after PM Edit lands so we know what the consumer page needs.
Do third · POD master
Lower leverage
POD SKU variants are flat catalog rows — current UI is decent. Touch only when packaging master is done and we have a consistent catalog-card pattern.
Why not all three at once? Two reasons. (1) Risk: changing 3 connected masters at the same time means more places for state to drift while we're mid-fix. (2) Feedback: the PM Edit redesign tells us what the consumer pages actually need. After you use the new PM Edit for a day, you'll know exactly what's missing from Packaging master and POD master — saving design rework on those.
