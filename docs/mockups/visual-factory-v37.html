Why OEE is shown as two numbers, not one composite

Standard OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality. We show A and Q honestly; P is the gap.

A · Availability
88%
Running minutes ÷ planned minutes. We track this — downtime is logged per machine.
P · Performance
—
Actual run rate ÷ machine nameplate rate. We don't track this — no per-machine nameplate rates, no hourly cycle data. Add P later when machine rates are reliable.
Q · Quality
95.8%
Good output ÷ total output. We track this via scrap %; Q = 100% − scrap%.

A composite that hides one of the three with a guessed P is worse than showing two real numbers. Until per-machine nameplate rates are entered as masters, OEE composite isn't trustworthy.
